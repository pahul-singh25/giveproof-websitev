How GiveProof Works

Three steps to transparent, accountable giving

1

Give

Browse verified campaigns created by vetted partner schools and NGOs. Match with a child whose story resonates, or onboard a child you personally know. Fund once or monthly.

2

Verify

Every campaign is backed by enrollment records, a structured budget, and guardian consent. GiveProof validates partner data with completeness checks and anomaly detection.

3

Proof

Receive a quarterly report for every child you fund — attendance, grades, teacher notes, consented photos, and receipts. See exactly where every dollar went.

What Makes GiveProof Different

No other platform combines all five of these features

📋

Verified Campaigns Only

Every campaign is created by a vetted partner organization with documentation and guardian consent. No unverified appeals. No guessing if the need is real.

📊

Quarterly Proof Reports

Structured reports with attendance, learning progress, teacher notes, photos, and receipts — generated by the platform and quality-checked before release.

🔒

No Custody of Funds

GiveProof never holds your money. Every donation flows through licensed payment processors directly to the serving partner organization.

🤝

Donor-Initiated Onboarding

Know a child who needs help? Bring them onto the platform yourself. GiveProof connects them with a verified partner for enrollment and reporting.

🌍

U.S. and International

In the U.S., campaigns cover supplies, tutoring, extracurriculars, and college access. Internationally, campaigns fund tuition, uniforms, books, and transport.

💰

Transparent Tiered Pricing

Our fee is visible before you give — and the more you donate, the less we take. You can choose to cover fees so 100% reaches the child.
